# Preliminary results and measurement procedures

## Current data release, 10 November 2026

The presentation in this directory is the 28 October research snapshot. Current measurements for the 454-video corpus, including three replacement sources and refreshed studio summaries, are available in the [10 November results release](../../../research/quantitative_exploration/corpus_refresh/20261110/README.md). Use that release for current corpus tables and figures. The dated presentation documents the measurements available when it was prepared.

Prepared 28 October 2026 with assistance from OpenAI Codex.

[Download the 24-slide Chinese PowerPoint](CinematicDX_研究结果汇报_20261028.pptx)

The presentation covers the available corpus, video-to-data procedures, local hard-cut validation, conservative interval sequences, digital gray measurements and independent narrative-event controls, studio-level comparisons, unsuccessful gradual-transition/face-proxy methods, and the current GUI. Text and tables are editable; existing plots and the real application screenshot are embedded images.

It reuses saved measurements and figures from the October 25–26 experiments and the repository audit. No film extraction was rerun for the slides. Per-slide speaker notes identify code, parameters, evidence paths and qualifications. Absolute local evidence paths in those notes refer to the researcher's workspace and are not public downloads.

The single-film visual–narrative result and the comparative studio results remain exploratory. Conditional control ranges are not confidence intervals. Detector candidates do not become verified shots or transitions through inclusion in the presentation. The underlying source videos and original annotations remain local.

The presentation was rendered and checked slide by slide, passed package/layout checks and was imported again for a final rendering. These checks do not imply native PowerPoint application testing, independent coauthor review or additional empirical validation.
